# /brag plan: Candor

Run with /brag-slim (Opus 5.5). Options: `--duration 75 --tone polished`, landscape 1920x1080, 30 fps.
75 s is three times the brag sweet spot, on purpose: this is the demo for a take-home, and the graders asked to see it working. Every answer, record and number on screen is real output or real data from the repo.

## Answers before planning

- **What is it?** A memory over two weeks of one person's work (meetings, dictation, Slack, email, calendar, Codex, ChatGPT) that answers questions as of any moment, with sources.
- **Who is it for?** Someone drowning in their own work history: a VP asking "what did we decide, and when?". Here, Alex Rivera, VP Product at Brightline. Today, the graders at Settle.
- **What sets it apart?** It knows *when*. The same question gets a different, correct answer depending on the moment you ask, and nothing from the future leaks in. It keeps first-hand and second-hand apart, and it says "I don't know".
- **Most impressive claim?** Retrieval 96%, answers 100% on the organizer's rules (89% with their LLM judge), actions 12/12, ₹0 spent. All measured.
- **Visual hook?** Three real messages that disagree about the launch date. Which one is true? It depends when you ask.
- **Real UI / flow to show?** Question, then the as-of moment, then the answer with record ids. The command that turns into a reminder on the calendar.
- **Tone?** Polished: dark, editorial, restrained motion, long holds, soft fades.
- **Share caption?** "Ask Candor when the launch is and it asks you back: as of when?"

## Angle

Time is the product. The video moves a playhead through two weeks and watches the truth change under it.

## Visual identity

- Ink background `#0b0c10` with soft vignette and film grain.
- New York (serif) for statements, SF Pro for UI, SF Mono for ids and timestamps.
- Amber `#f5b14c` for what is true now, cyan `#7dd3fc` for the as-of playhead, grey `#8b8f98` for history.
- Source tags keep one color each: meeting rose, Slack violet, email teal, calendar amber, dictation lime.

## Sound

An original piece synthesized for this video: A minor, 92 BPM. Warm pad (Am, Fmaj7, C, G), a soft sub, and a plucked arpeggio from the reveal on. A light pulse under scenes 3 to 6 drops out for the close. The effects are in the same key and sit under the music: soft key ticks for typing, a chime on A/E when an answer lands, airy swells on scene changes, a muted pluck for the reminder pin.
